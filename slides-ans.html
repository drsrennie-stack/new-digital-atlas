Autonomic Nervous System

The autonomic nervous system controls involuntary function through two divisions: the sympathetic (fight or flight) and the parasympathetic (rest and digest). This deck builds the two-neuron pathway, the ganglia and routes of each division, and their effects on the body. Dr. Sharilyn Rennie
