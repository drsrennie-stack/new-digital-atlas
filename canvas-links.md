# Canvas links, plain and simple
Two ways to add each one. **External URL is easier** and needs no HTML.

**A. Module item, no HTML.** In the Canvas module, click **+**, choose
**External URL**, paste the URL, name it, and tick **Load in a new tab**.

**B. On a page.** Paste the HTML in the `</>` editor.

The `?sec=` part is the one thing worth not dropping. It bakes the section
into the page, which is what keeps Safari students from being asked to pick
their section every single visit.
