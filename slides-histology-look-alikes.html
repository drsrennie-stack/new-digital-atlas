Module 1 · Histology

Look-Alikes

Half of your Exam 1 practical is microscopy. You will not lose those points because you cannot say the words. You will lose them because two slides look the same and you pick the wrong one.

So today we build the thing that tells them apart, one feature at a time.

2

Why this block, today

You already know the words. That is not what a practical tests.

Memorising names is real work, and it is the part your recall cards already do better across five nights than we could do in one hour. So we are not spending the hour on it.

What a room buys you, and cards cannot, is telling apart two things that look the same at an odd plane of section. That is a looking skill, and it only improves against pairs.

3

How today runs

Three moves

1
Find the deciding feature. Every option you see is a true statement. Only one of them decides the pair.
2
Say why the others fail. Three near-misses, three different reasons. This is the part that shows up on the practical.
3
Take it to the bench. Your key goes with you into lab, where the same tissues are under a scope.
4

Get your phone out

Join the room

Scan it, or type the address and enter the code. Nothing you send has your name on it, and nothing here is graded.

5

The rule for every question today

All four options are true. Only one of them decides it.

A feature that is true of both tissues cannot separate them, however correct it is. That is the trap, and it is the one that costs points, because a true statement feels like an answer.

Ask what would let you call it in five seconds, on a slide you have never seen.

Move two

A near-miss fails for one of three reasons

Naming which one it is takes about four seconds and it is the difference between remembering an answer and being able to work one out.

Now, at the bench

Take your key to lab

Work the rest of the pairs on the activity page and print your key. It is the only page you should need at the scope.

Every pair you finish there adds one line to it.

●
I never saw the featureA looking problem. Back to the scope with a slide you already named right.
●
I named the feature wrongA vocabulary problem. That one is tonight's cards, not this room.
●
Right feature, wrong tissueA key problem. Your deciding feature was not deciding. Rewrite that line.

Before Thursday

Finish the pairs, then let the cards do the names

The activity page holds all of them. The ones we did not poll are the ones you are most likely to meet on the practical, so do not skip to the end.
